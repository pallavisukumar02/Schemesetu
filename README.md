# SchemeSetu — AI-Powered Government Scheme Discovery & Readiness Platform

SchemeSetu helps entrepreneurs and individuals discover government schemes they may be eligible for, understand their benefits, assess their application readiness, and prepare the documents required to apply.

The platform combines personalized scheme matching, AI-assisted guidance, application tracking, nearby support discovery, and multilingual access in one place.

**Core product flow**
1. Create a personalized profile.
2. Discover relevant government schemes.
3. Understand eligibility and matching reasons.
4. Check application readiness.
5. Prepare personalized document checklists.
6. Explore business and project opportunities.
7. Compare government schemes.
8. Discover nearby banks and support services.
9. Track application progress.
10. Access multilingual AI assistance.

**Technology:** React, Node.js, Express, SQLite, AI integrations, OpenStreetMap and Leaflet.
